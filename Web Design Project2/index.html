About Us

Executive Summary

Frameworks Pty Ltd is a South African sports infrastructure company founded by Luthando Velleman. We specialize in the development, restoration, and maintenance of sports facilities for High Schools, TVET Colleges, Universities, and Rural Communities across South Africa. We believe a proper field is not a luxury. It is where discipline is learned, talent is discovered, and communities come together

We create safe, professional, and match-ready enviroments where athletes can train, compete, and pursue their ambitions with confidence.

Our goal is to provide professional, affordable and reliable solutions.

Why Frameworks?

➔ We Understand Institutions.

We know how schools and public colleges work - SGB approvals, tender processes, tight budgets and the need for a facility that lasts. We do it right the first time, with no hidden costs.

➔ We Build to Last, Not Just to Look Good.

We don't just paint a court. We fix the drainage underneath, we use proper SABS-approved materials, and we build for heavy use in South African weather.

➔ We Develop More Than Facilities.

Every project creates local work. We hire locally where possible and train young people in basic construction and maintenance skills, so the facility can be maintained long after we leave.

Our Services

➢ Site clearing and earthworks

➢ Professional levelling and drainage systems

➢ Grass laying (natural and synthetic turf)

➢ Hard courts construction and resurfacing

➢ Fencing, lighting, and ablution facilities

➢ Line marking, goalposts, and sports equipment supply

➢ Ongoing maintenance plans for schools and colleges

High Schools

Full construction and rehabilitation of soccer fields, rugby fields, netball and basketball courts, athletics tracks, and cricket pitches.

Includes drainage, fencing, goalposts, and line marking to SASFA and departmental standards.

TVET Colleges & Universities

Upgrading of sporting infrastructure at tertiary institutes for varsity sports.

We build facilities that meet USSA and federation standards for hosting tournaments, while also being durable enough for daily use.

Rural Communities

Our core passion.

We develop community sports grounds, multi-use combi courts, and playground facilities that are low-maintenance, vandal-resistant, and built to last in harsh conditions.

We work with municipalities, ward councilors and local SGBs to deliver projects that the whole community can enjoy.
